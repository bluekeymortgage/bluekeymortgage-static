Why you can't borrow it all, and why that protects you

Canadian reverse mortgage lenders cap what you can borrow, at most about 55% of your home's value, and less at younger ages. That cap isn't stinginess. It's the reason lenders can guarantee you'll never owe more than your home is worth, and it means a healthy share of your home's value stays yours as equity on day one.

The money you unlock is tax-free, because it's your own equity, not income. It generally doesn't affect Old Age Security or the Guaranteed Income Supplement either.

What decides your real number

  • The age of the youngest owner, older means more.
  • Your home's appraised value, type, and location, urban homes in strong markets qualify for more.
  • Which lender, HomeEquity Bank (CHIP), Equitable Bank (Flex), and Bloom each set their own limits. I compare all three for you, that's my job.

A reverse mortgage is a loan secured against your home. It must be repaid when you sell your home, move out permanently, or pass away. Interest accrues over time and reduces the equity remaining in your home. Terms, conditions, and lender qualification apply; approval, rates, and amounts are never guaranteed. A reverse mortgage is not a government program or benefit.

The information on this website is general education, not legal, tax, or investment advice. Please consult an independent legal or financial advisor about your personal situation. Independent legal advice is required before completing any reverse mortgage in Ontario.
