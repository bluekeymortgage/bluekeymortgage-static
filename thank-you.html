A reverse mortgage is a loan secured against your home. It must be repaid when you sell your home, move out permanently, or pass away. Interest accrues over time and reduces the equity remaining in your home. Terms, conditions, and lender qualification apply; approval, rates, and amounts are never guaranteed. A reverse mortgage is not a government program or benefit.

The information on this website is general education, not legal, tax, or investment advice. Please consult an independent legal or financial advisor about your personal situation. Independent legal advice is required before completing any reverse mortgage in Ontario.
